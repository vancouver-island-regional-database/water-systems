On March 31, 2020, Ministry of Environment and Climate Change Strategy, Environmental Protection Division staff
conducted an inspection of the Town of Ladysmith's wastewater treatment facility located at 245 A Oyster Cove Road,
Ladysmith, BC with authorization number 120 under the Environmental Management Act.
This Advisory, the alleged violations and the circumstances to which it refers will form part of the compliance history of
Town of Ladysmith, and will be taken into account in the event of future non-compliance.
Please note that this authorization is considered to be out of compliance until such a time as it can be confirmed to meet
the authorization requirements.

Details/Findings:

On March 31, 2020, Ministry of Environment and Climate Change Strategy (Ministry)
Officer Connor Fraleigh (Officer Fraleigh) conducted an office review inspection of the
Town of Ladysmith wastewater treatment facility (Facility) located in Ladysmith, BC to
verify compliance with Permit 120 (Permit). The Permit authorizes the discharge of effluent
to Ladysmith Harbour from a municipal sewage system. The Permit was first issued on
March 12, 1965, and last amended on January 10, 1994.
The inspection period for this report is from January 1, 2019 to December 31, 2019 and
included a review of the following documents:
- 2019 Quarterly Water Quality Data Reports (2019 Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4 WQ DATA)
- 2019 Summary of Bypasses at the Wastewater Treatment Plant report was submitted to
the Ministry on January 10, 2020 (2019 Bypass Report)
A review of the 2019 WQ Data confirmed that the Town of Ladysmith exceeded the
maximum allowable rate of discharge on the following dates:
January 3, 2019 - 11,240 m3/d
January 4, 2019 - 9,424 m3/d
Flow rates for the remainder of the inspection period were within the allowable maximum
daily discharge rate.

Compliance:

Out

(BOD5) and Total Suspended Solids (TSS) were well below permitted limits. The maximum

Requirement Description:

2. GENERAL REQUIREMENTS, 2.1 Maintenance of Works and Emergency
Procedures
2.1: The Permittee shall inspect the pollution control works regularly and maintain them in
good working order. In the event of an emergency or condition beyond the control of the
Permittee which prevents continuing operation of the approved method of pollution control,
the Permittee shall immediately notify the Regional Waste Manager and take appropriate
remedial action.

Details/Findings:

The requirement to maintain the authorized works was not assessed as part of this office
review inspection. Therefore, compliance with this requirement was not applicable.

Compliance:

Not Applicable

Requirement Description:

2. GENERAL REQUIREMENTS, 2.2 Bypasses
2.2: The discharge of effluent which has bypassed the designated treatment works is
prohibited unless the consent of the Regional Waste Manager is obtained and confirmed in
writing.

Details/Findings:

Page one of the 2019 Bypass Report states that there were no bypass events to report in
2019. Therefore, compliance with this requirement is not applicable throughout the
inspection period.

Compliance:

Not Applicable

Requirement Description:

2. GENERAL REQUIREMENTS, 2.5 Outfall Inspection
2.5: The Permittee shall conduct a dye test on the outfall lines (or inspect by another
method approved by the Regional Waste Manager) every five years or as may otherwise
be required by the Regional Waste Manager.

Details/Findings:

The last dye test conducted on the Town of Ladysmith's wastewater treatment plant outfalls
was completed on February 21, 2018. Therefore, compliance with this requirement was not
applicable throughout the inspection period.

Requirement Description:

3. MONITORING AND REPORTING REQUIREMENTS, 3.1 Discharge Monitoring, 3.1.1
Sampling and Analysis
3.1.1: The Permittee shall install a suitable sampling facility and obtain a composite sample
of the effluent from the treatment plant effluent once every month. The sample is to consist
of four grab samples taken over a two-hour period at maximum flow and mixed to form a
single sample for subsequent analysis. Proper care should be taken in sampling, storing
and transporting the sample to adequately control temperature and avoid contamination,
breakage, etc.
Obtain analyses of the sample for the following:
5-day Biochemical Oxygen Demand;
Total Suspended Solids.

Details/Findings:

Following a review of the 2019 WQ Data, it was confirmed that sampling and analyses
were performed for 5-day Biochemical Oxygen Demand and Total Suspended Solids as
required. Fecal coliforms samples were also collected an analyzed on a monthly basis.
Sample analysis was performed by Maxxam Analytics and ALS Environmental of Burnaby,
BC.

Compliance:

In

Flow Measurement
3.1.2: Provide and maintain suitable flow measuring devices and record once per day the
effluent volume discharged over a 24-hour period from the sewage treatment plant and the
overflow bypass.

Details/Findings:
