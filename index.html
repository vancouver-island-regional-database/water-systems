Water Systems: Marine & Freshwater Records

This collection brings together hard-to-find information about water systems from multiple official websites. Public records and data related to drinking water, wastewater, marine water quality, fisheries, and environmental monitoring are presented in accessible formats, inlcuding chronological timelines and visually graphed datasets, with direct links and mirrored backup copies to ensure continued access in case the original sources become unavailable.

                  Ladysmith Harbour Water Quality Graphs

                  Ladysmith Harbour's water quality is presented here as visual graphs in order to facilitate a better understanding of technical water quality datasets through accessible formatting.* The data has been aggregated directly from government and regional-health monitoring records and no data points have been omitted, adjusted, or reweighed. Incomplete datasets due to gaps in the available public record have been indicated and the appended descriptions reflect objective visual elements and basic details are included without commentary or opinion in order to support accessibility for people with vision impairment.

                  * In accordance with the Accessible British Columbia Act, SBC 2021, c 19, s 2(1).

                  Questions about these charts
                  What do these charts measure?

                  "Both methods of testing, CBOD and microbiological testing, serve different purposes. Microbiological testing is more focused on public health since it identifies the presence of harmful pathogens in the effluent. CBOD monitoring is more focused on protecting the environment and aquatic life."

                  • 2023 Federal ammendments to the Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations, IDEXX Laboratories Inc. commentary
                  Who monitors water quality at Transfer Beach?

                  "The Vancouver Island Health Authority (VIHA) has a program to routinely monitor recreational beaches within its jurisdiction. Beaches are monitored to protect swimmers from illnesses that may be linked to unacceptable bacteria levels."

                  • Town of Ladysmith, Liquid Waste Management Plan Stages 1 & 2, p. 3-17 (PDF p. 49)
                  • Sample results: Island Health, Transfer Beach North
                  • Sample results: Island Health, Transfer Beach South
                  What causes shellfish harvesting closures in Ladysmith Harbour?

                  "The Ladysmith WWTP has reported a sample of their treated effluent that was above the threshold established for the safe harvest of bivalve shellfish."1
